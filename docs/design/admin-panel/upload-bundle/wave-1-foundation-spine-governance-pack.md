### 1.3 Approval Queue (unified work list; two-court)
One queue over onboarding, voucher, merchant-edit and branch-lifecycle approvals, split into TWO COURTS (owner-decided D37) so it is always clear whose action is being waited on: "Needs you" (the operator's court: unclaimed + claimed-by-you) and "Awaiting merchant" (the merchant's court: changes-requested, and items awaiting the merchant's approval of a material correction). Show the courts as tabs, plus a per-row whose-court pill so a mixed view still reads correctly. On "Awaiting merchant" rows the waiting-age tracks how long the MERCHANT has had the item; when the merchant acts, the item returns to "Needs you". (NET-NEW: the two-court tabs, the whose-court pill, and the merchant-side waiting-age are part of the net-new correction flow; today's queue is single-court and operator-centric. Design them faithfully as the target but do not imply they are already built.) Columns: merchant, type, waiting-age (age-tinted, neutral/amber/red, oldest-first, NO SLA countdown), verification, status, owner/claim, whose-court. Status-filter chips with counts. 45s freshness + manual refresh + "last updated". A ">24h claimed" stale flag. States: loading (skeleton rows + count skeleton), empty ("no items match", per court), error (distinct, retry), permission-denied (a role without approval:read), responsive collapse.

## Wave 1 clickable flows
- Two-court queue: "Needs you" tab to claim to review; then either COSMETIC correct-on-behalf (apply directly, item stays in your court to approve-and-go-live) OR MATERIAL correct-on-behalf (voucher value / saving, business / trading name to sent-to-merchant, item moves to "Awaiting merchant") with each result state (D37).
